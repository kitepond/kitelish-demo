        How every project runs

        • 1

          Ask

          We start with a real question, such as how to make a kite stay up, and the words needed to discuss it.

        • 2

          Build

          Teams plan and build with simple, safe materials. The teacher speaks English and children answer in English as best they can.

        • 3

          Test

          We try it, measure it and write down what happened, even when the first version does not work.

        • 4

          Present

          Every child explains one thing they learned, in a few sentences, to the group.

        Parents

        What parents might say

        Questions parents ask

        Does my child need good English to start?

        No. We place children by age and a short conversation, then give them the words they need for each build.

        Is this a science class or an English class?

        Both. The project is the reason to speak: every lesson has a build, a science idea and a speaking goal.

        What happens in the free trial?

        Your child joins one normal session in the stage we suggest. Afterwards we share how it went and what we would recommend.

        How many children are in a group?

        Groups are small, for example up to ten children. Set your own limit and show it here.
