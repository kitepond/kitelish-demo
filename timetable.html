Term plan

Twelve weeks, four project blocks

A sample plan for one term. Each block has one big build and a short list of English words that go with it.

Sample plan. Replace the weeks, projects and words with your own curriculum.

Weeks 1–3Wind and flight

Children ask what keeps a kite in the air, then build and fly their own.

What they make A paper kite with a tail

Key words
  • wind
  • lift
  • string
Weeks 4–6Bridges and loads

Compare shapes, add weight and record which design holds the most.

What they make A paper bridge that holds coins

Key words
  • beam
  • load
  • strong
Weeks 7–9Machines that move

Wheels, gears and wind power: build something that turns and count the spins.

What they make A windmill or a rolling cart

Key words
  • wheel
  • spin
  • power
Weeks 10–12Showcase

Choose a favourite project, improve it and present it to families.

What they make A final build and a short talk

Key words
  • improve
  • present
  • because
