Launch, Lift and Soar

Three stages for ages 6 to 11. Each one grows the STEM idea and the English that goes with it.

The flight path

Three stages, one flight path

Children move up a stage when they are ready, not just when they have a birthday. Every stage mixes a build, a science idea and a short talk in English.

  1. Ages 6–7

    Stage 1 · Launch

    First builds

    Simple builds with big parts, guided questions and a lot of speaking practice with words children can use right away.

    • Name the parts of a build in English
    • Say what they think will happen
    • Show the finished build in two or three sentences
    Book a Launch trial
  2. Ages 8–9

    Stage 2 · Lift

    Systems and tests

    Builds with moving parts. Children measure, compare two versions and write short lab notes together.

    • Measure and record results in a table
    • Compare two designs using because
    • Work in a team and share jobs politely
    Book a Lift trial
  3. Ages 10–11

    Stage 3 · Soar

    Own design and demo

    Children plan their own project, solve problems that come up and present to an audience at the end of term.

    • Plan a project with steps and a materials list
    • Explain a problem and how they fixed it
    • Give a short demo and answer questions
    Book a Soar trial
